Domo Named a Leader in the 2025 Nucleus Research Analytics Technology Value Matrix (, 2025-07-15)
Source: https://www.domo.com/news/press/domo-named-a-leader-in-the-2025-nucleus-research-analytics-technology-value-matrix
Summary: SILICON SLOPES, Utah — Today Domo (Nasdaq: DOMO) announced it has been named a leader in Nucleus Research’s 2025 Business Intelligence (BI) and Analytics Technology Value Matrix for the fifth consecutive year. Nucleus Research recognized Domo for its ability to combine advanced analytics, interactive dashboards, and near real-time visualizations that help to transform data into actionable insights. The firm also noted that Domo’s over 1,000 pre-built connectors help to unify data from ERPs, CRMs, and cloud systems.
