Summary: Unstructured data opportunities in marketing

“To be honest, so much of the data that we were working with in marketing for years and years was a relatively narrow set of things that were well structured,” Scott says.

 Unstructured data in the marketing context, on the other hand, includes things such as email threads, chatbot transcripts and any text documents or even visual data that are not easily organized in a traditional database. “So much of that data just never really was in a form that marketers were able to harness or mine or leverage,” Scott says.
